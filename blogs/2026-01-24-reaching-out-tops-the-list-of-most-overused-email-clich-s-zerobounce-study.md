Santa Barbara, CA – Jan. 19, 2026 – “Reaching out” is the most overused email cliché, with “checking in” and “follow up” close behind, according to a new study by ZeroBounce. Phrases like “please advise” and “hope my email finds you well” are still prevalent in corporate communication, even as AI tools become more common […]
